What the project demonstrates

  • Packet delivery ratio = successfully delivered original packets ÷ total packets × 100%.
  • Retransmissions = additional transmissions after the first attempt.
  • Bandwidth usage is represented by total packet transmissions (original packets + retries).
  • Average delay is a simplified simulated end-to-end delay, not a measurement from a real network.
  • ACK means acknowledgement; NACK means negative acknowledgement; ARQ means Automatic Repeat reQuest.

Academic honesty note: this is a teaching simulation with a lightweight logistic risk heuristic, not a production-grade trained AI model or a real network benchmark. In your presentation, describe its assumptions and limitations clearly.
